# Business Guidelines

## Product Positioning

Working Hours Tracker is positioned as a practical, trustworthy daily operations tool for knowledge-work tracking and reporting.

## Decision Principles

- Prioritize reliability over speculative feature breadth.
- Preserve backward-compatible exports whenever possible.
- Keep user effort low for repetitive workflows.

## Governance Principles

- Every behavior change must have documentation and traceability impact updates.
- KPI/OKR alignment is required for medium/large roadmap changes.
- High-risk workflow changes require explicit risk and mitigation notes.
